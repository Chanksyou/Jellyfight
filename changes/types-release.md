Jelly types are here: after your first run you pick a jelly (Normal, Fire, Lightning, Ice, Poison, Wind, Glitter or Tentacle) before every run, each with its own attack, look and 12 Attack upgrades (one after each of the first three bosses). New types unlock by finishing acts and defeating Elites. The old Element treasures and the separate evolution pick are replaced: evolutions now come in the Legendary pick after a boss.
