# Cross-tier performance optimizations

- **GPU & shaders (all tiers):** Removed accidental `transmission` on Clog scum and Ice Cube gadget materials (eliminating extra full-scene transmission passes); gated `critters.js` normal maps on Low graphics; tagged non-lightmapped furniture with `lightUv = -1` and skipped zero-weight axes in `gridLight()`; deduplicated `worldN`/`lbN` and reused `lb.rgb` for horizontal floor mist in `baked-ao.js`/`mist.js`; culled inactive mist holes/vortices/trail segments; precomputed `DOF_OFFSETS` and linearized CoC math in `graphics.js`, inlining `finite()` to remove the redundant High `FiniteShader` pass; combined `BloomOutputPass` saturation/grey into a single dot+mix; uniform-branched `skyline.js` and `rain.js` fragment shaders; switched `VolumeMaterial` and `shark-water.js` to `THREE.FrontSide`; reduced bubble and shower curtain tessellation.
- **VFX & boss idle pools:** Hid idle `InstancedMesh`/`Points`/`Mesh` pools across `systems.js`, `vfx.js`, `ice-looks.js`, `wind-looks.js`, `glitter-looks.js`, `acid-looks.js`, `encore-floor.js`, `encore-contact.js`, `encore-wall.js`, `encore-pyro.js`, `encore-records.js`, `encore.js`, `shark.js`, `vacuum.js`, and `vent-air.js`; added `addUpdateRange()` partial uploads to `batch.js`, `systems.js`, `vfx.js`, `shark.js`, and `bubble-looks.js`; dirty-checked `AirLight` uploads; cached world bounding spheres in `static-batch.js`; added `Box3` broadphase to `encore-contact.js`; moved `treasure-glow.js` noise/ripple work inside active `uKind` branches; cached `Clog._ropeLut` trig table.
- **CPU loop, collision, AI & HUD:** Memoized `LOOK.num()`/`LOOK.color()`; cached inverse collider matrices and pruned BVH/triangle checks in `collision.js`; hoisted chain segment lengths in `chain.js`/`tentacles.js`; cached elite halos and batched enemy separation moves in `enemies.js`; replaced `get mods()` template key and dirty-checked `setItems()` in `run.js`; replaced V8 dictionary-mode `delete` pool resets in `bubbles.js`; cached static HUD DOM refs and minimap `Path2D` in `hud.js`; skipped hidden accent style writes in `fx.js`; skipped 3D apartment stepping while `TypePicker` is open.
- **Startup & memory:** Parallelized `loadLook()`/`loadContent()`, `meshoptDecoder()`/`apartment.json`, `loadBakedAO` fetches, multi-part act GLB downloads, and unique `.webp` surface texture loads; deferred synthesized `Music` creation until needed; deduplicated `uploadAll` texture uploads and scissored Low warm-up draws to 4×4; disposed temporary room environment, character halo, and set-dressing merge geometries.
